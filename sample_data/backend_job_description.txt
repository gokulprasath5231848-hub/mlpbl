Job Title: Backend Developer

We are hiring a Backend Developer to join the platform team. The successful
candidate will have strong experience with Java, Spring Boot, REST APIs and
SQL, and will be comfortable working against PostgreSQL in production.

Experience with Docker and Git is expected. Exposure to Kubernetes and Linux
administration is preferred but not essential.

You will own services end to end, take part in design reviews, and work
closely with the frontend and data teams.
